# StudyNotes-Hub
A free educational platform where students can access study notes and learning resources.
📚 StudyNotes Hub

Your Learning, Your Notes, Your Success.

About the Project

StudyNotes Hub is a free educational platform designed to help school, college, and university students access study notes and learning resources easily.

Our goal is to make education simple, accessible, and free for everyone.

Project Objectives

- Provide free educational notes.
- Help students understand difficult topics.
- Organize learning resources.
- Support students in exam preparation.

Key Features

- Free study notes.
- Student-friendly interface.
- Easy navigation.
- Educational resources.
- Subject-based learning materials.

AI Tool Used

- Lovable AI – Website development.
- ChatGPT – Prompt generation and project documentation.
